Money & contracts

"What will this really cost us?"

Are there hidden costs, add-ons, or hourly overruns?

No. Every package is a fixed price agreed in writing before work starts. If the scope ever needs to change — because you ask for something extra — I quote that separately and you approve it before anything happens. You will never open a surprise invoice from me. Hourly billing is not part of my business.

How does payment work? Can we pay by purchase order or cheque?

Yes to both. Typical terms: 50% deposit to book your dates, 50% on completion. I accept cheque, e-transfer, and municipal purchase orders — whatever your standard process is. I provide proper invoices with GST numbers that your administrator can process without any fuss.

What if the project goes over the quoted scope?

It can't — that's what "fixed scope in writing" means. If something genuinely unforeseen comes up (rare), I stop, explain it in plain language, and give you options at fixed prices. You decide. Work never continues on unbilled assumptions.

What if we're not satisfied with the work?

I make it right. If delivered work doesn't match the written scope, I fix it at no charge until it does. And because the scope was written down before we started, there's nothing to argue about. In practice: I'd rather over-deliver than have an unhappy council — my whole business runs on word of mouth in small communities.

What if our budget is smaller than the listed prices?

Tell me at the assessment. I've never turned away a small village over money — a scoped-down version of the package, a payment schedule that fits your budget year, or a smaller starting point that grows later are all on the table. The fixed prices cover standard scopes; small budgets get real flexibility.

Risk & responsibility

"What could go wrong — and who's on the hook?"

Is this legal advice? What if we rely on it and something goes wrong?

I provide practical privacy-program, records, and technology work based on published legislation and official guidance — not legal advice, and I say so clearly on every page and document. Your municipality's legal advisor confirms what applies to your situation. I work alongside your advisor gladly. I don't overstate what I do — that protects you and me.

Do you carry insurance / professional liability coverage?

This is a fair question for any vendor and I answer it directly at the assessment. As a sole proprietor I keep overhead lean — which is exactly why my prices beat the big firms — and I'll walk you through what that means for your risk management, including any terms your municipality requires in a vendor contract. If your procurement rules require specific coverage, tell me early and we'll figure out the right structure together.

What if you get sick, hit by a bus, or disappear mid-project?

The honest one-person-shop question — and you deserve a real answer. Three safeguards: (1) All work-in-progress files and documentation belong to you and live in your systems, not just mine; (2) Everything is written down as we go — another professional could pick up from the documentation; (3) Work is staged in deliverables, so you're never far from a usable state. I also keep relationships with other independent professionals I can bring in if needed.

What about procurement rules? Can a village even hire you directly?

Small purchases under your municipality's threshold can typically be approved administratively — and these package prices usually sit well under the thresholds that require open bidding. If your municipality has a specific policy, quote me the number and I'll structure the engagement to fit. If a formal process is needed, I provide written quotes and documentation formatted for council agendas.

Trust & data

"What happens to our information — and why trust you?"

What happens to our records and data while you work with us?

Your data stays yours. Project files are stored encrypted and are handed over or securely deleted at project end, your choice. I never share, sell, or reuse municipal information — and I'm happy to sign your confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement before we start. Notably, this website's chat assistant runs entirely in your own browser — nothing you type to it ever leaves your computer.

You're one person. Why not hire a big firm?

Big firms do good work — at big-firm prices, with junior staff learning on your file and a rotating cast of contacts. With me you get the person who does the work, directly, for a fraction of the cost. For a village-sized project, a focused individual beats an overhead-heavy firm on price, speed, and attention. The trade-off is capacity: I take on a limited number of clients at a time so nobody waits.

How do we know you'll actually deliver?

Three concrete reasons: (1) Work is staged in deliverables you approve — you see progress, not promises; (2) Payment follows delivery, not the other way around (beyond the booking deposit); (3) Ask me for references from past work — and the free assessment itself is a no-risk sample of how I operate before you commit a dollar.

What if we already have some of this in place — will you just sell us more?

No. The free assessment maps what you already have versus what you actually need. You only pay for gaps. If you're already in good shape, I'll tell you — and the written gap summary is yours to keep. A vendor who says "you don't need me for that part" is exactly the vendor you want.

Process & logistics

"How will this actually work day to day?"

How much of our staff's time will this take?

Less than you fear. The assessment needs about an hour. After that, I do the heavy lifting and come to you with focused questions — typically a few short check-ins rather than endless meetings. Training is a single scheduled half-day for the Privacy & Records Package. I know your staff have five other jobs; the process is designed around that.

What if our staff aren't tech-comfortable?

Perfect — that's normal, and it's who I design for. Training is plain-language and hands-on, documentation is written for humans (not IT departments), and I stay available after handover for questions. If your administrator can handle email, they can handle what I deliver.

Do we need to be in a city? Do you come out to us?

I come to you — anywhere in Alberta. Assessments and kickoffs are on-site whenever it helps; the rest works well by phone, email, or video. Being remote is not a problem; being rural is not a problem. That's the whole market I serve.

What exactly do we end up owning at the end?

Everything. Documents, templates, records systems, website files, logins — all of it, in your municipality's name and accounts. No lock-in, no hostage situations, no proprietary formats you can't open. If you decide to hire someone else after me, they'll find everything in order.

What happens after the project is done?

Your choice. Websites include a year of updates, with a simple optional care plan after that. The Full Compliance Partner includes a 6-month review and priority support. For anything else, you can call me any time — existing clients get my best response times. There's no obligation to keep me on; plenty of villages just keep my number for when something comes up.

Can you work with our existing lawyer / accountant / consultant?

Gladly. I plug into whatever team you have. Your lawyer confirms legal questions, I handle the practical build, and everyone gets the documentation they need. No ego, no turf wars — the goal is your village covered properly.
