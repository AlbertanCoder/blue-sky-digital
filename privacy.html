Privacy Policy

Your information, plainly explained.

A privacy business should have an honest privacy policy. Here's mine — in the same plain language I'd want to read myself.

What I collect

Only what you choose to give me through the free assessment form or by email: your name, your municipality, contact details, and whatever you include in your message. That's it.

What I don't collect

  • No tracking cookies. No advertising pixels. No analytics that follow you around.
  • No selling, sharing, or trading of information — to anyone, for any reason.
  • No access to your municipal systems or records unless we have a written engagement and you grant it.

How I use it

Exactly one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and, if you hire me, to do the work we agreed to in writing. I may keep basic business records (quotes, invoices, correspondence) for accounting and legal purposes, as any business must.

How it's protected & stored

Inquiry emails and business records are stored with established Canadian and international email/business providers with industry-standard security. Project files for municipal clients are stored encrypted, and I'm glad to sign your municipality's confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement before any engagement.

How long I keep it

Inquiries that don't become projects: deleted within 12 months. Client business records: kept for the period required by tax and business law, then securely deleted. If you want your inquiry deleted sooner, just ask.

Your rights

You can ask what information I hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion — email hello@blueskydigital.ca and I'll respond promptly. Alberta's privacy laws give individuals rights regarding their personal information, and I respect them fully.

The form on this website

The assessment form delivers your message directly to my business email. If the email service is ever unavailable, the form falls back to your own email program so you stay in control of what's sent.

Plain truth: I'm in the privacy business because I believe small organizations deserve to handle people's information well. That starts with my own practices. If anything here is ever unclear, ask me — a real person answers.

This policy covers Blue Sky Digital's own website and business practices. Client projects are governed by their individual written agreements. Last updated: October 2026.
