Privacy Policy

Your information, plainly explained.

A privacy-focused business should have an honest privacy policy. Here's mine.

What I collect

Only what you choose to give me — the assessment form or an email: name, municipality, contact details, and whatever you write. The quote quiz asks questions but requires no personal information and stores nothing.

About the AI assistant & estimator

The AI on this site is powered by Puter (a third-party AI service). When you chat or take the quote quiz, your messages are processed by that service to generate an answer — the same as any AI chat tool. Don't type private municipal or personal data into it. The free assessment (human-to-human) is where sensitive details belong.

What I don't do

No tracking cookies. No advertising pixels. No selling, sharing, or trading information — to anyone, for any reason.

How it's protected & kept

Business correspondence lives with established email providers with industry-standard security. Client project files are stored encrypted. Inquiries are deleted within 12 months if they don't become projects. Want yours deleted sooner? Just ask.

Your rights

Ask what I hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion — email hello@rockymountaindigital.ca and a real person responds. Alberta's privacy laws give individuals rights regarding their personal information, and I respect them fully.

Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers this website and general business practices; client projects are governed by individual written agreements.
