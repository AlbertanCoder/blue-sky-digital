About Blue Sky Digital

One person. Full attention. Honest work.

I'm Shad Hunter, a self-taught software developer. I learned the values this business runs on the long way: show up when you say you will, quote the job straight, and never do work you wouldn't put your name on.

Why villages?

Because that's where the gap is. Alberta's new privacy expectations (POPA and the updated ATIA) land hardest on the smallest offices — the ones with one administrator, a part-time CAO, and a council of volunteers. The big firms know it, and they quote big-firm prices.

I built Blue Sky Digital to be the practical middle ground: real expertise, real deliverables, and prices a village budget can actually absorb. When you hire me, you get me — the person who did the work, sitting in the room with your council explaining it.

How I work

Plain language. If I can't explain it so a councillor can follow along, I haven't finished my job.

Fixed prices. You'll never get a surprise invoice from me. Scope is written down first.

I show up. In person at your office whenever it matters. Rural Alberta isn't a "remote only" market — it's a relationships market.

"Big-city consulting quotes put small municipalities in a hard position. Same obligations, a fraction of the budget. I exist to fix that equation."

— Shad Hunter
