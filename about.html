About me

Self-taught. Ships like a team.

I'm Shad Hunter. I taught myself software development and now build civic tech for Alberta's small municipalities — the kind of tools that usually cost ten times more. When you work with me, you get me: the person who designs it, builds it, tests it, and explains it to your council.

🧠

How I actually work

AI-assisted engineering, honestly done. I direct modern AI tools to move fast — then verify every layer myself: tests, security, data, deployment. The speed is AI. The judgment and the accountability are mine.

🎯

Why villages?

New privacy rules land hardest on the smallest offices — one administrator wearing five hats. Big firms quote big-firm prices. I built this practice to be the practical middle ground: real capability, fair prices.

🔧

What I bring

Full-stack development (frontend, backend, databases), encryption & security, LLM/AI integration, research & compliance documentation, plain-language training — and working prototypes, not promises.

Built something you need? Or building a team?

I take on projects for Alberta communities — and I'm always open to conversations with good teams who need someone that ships. Both doors lead to the same inbox.
