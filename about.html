About

One person. Full attention.

I'm a self-taught developer who builds practical tech, privacy, and records solutions for Alberta's small municipalities. When you hire me, you get me — the person who does the work, sitting in the room with your council explaining it.

🤝

Why villages?

Because that's where the gap is. New privacy rules land hardest on the smallest offices — one administrator wearing five hats. Big firms quote big-firm prices. I built this to be the practical middle ground.

💬

How I work

Plain language, always. Fixed scope in writing. I show up in person when it matters. If I can't explain it so a councillor can follow along, I haven't finished my job.

🏔️

Service area

Anywhere in Alberta. Assessments and kickoffs on-site whenever it helps; phone, email, or video for the rest. Being rural isn't a problem — it's the whole market.
