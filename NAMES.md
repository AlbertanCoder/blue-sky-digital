### How to pick
- **Say it on the phone** 5× fast. Can your village administrator spell it back?
- **Google it** + check Alberta's business name registry (NUANS) before committing.
- **Domain check** (.ca and .com) — many .ca domains for these are likely available.
- The name matters less than the work — the site is built so swapping names takes 5 minutes (it's a find-and-replace across 5 files + `js/ai.js` context).
